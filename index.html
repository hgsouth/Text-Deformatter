Your Privacy is Protected

This tool runs entirely in your web browser. When you paste text, it's processed locally on your device using JavaScript—nothing is uploaded to any server. Your text never leaves your computer, and we don't store, track, or have access to anything you paste here.

You can verify this yourself: open your browser's developer tools and check the Network tab while using the app. You'll see zero requests being sent. This app even works offline once loaded, proving that no data is being transmitted.
